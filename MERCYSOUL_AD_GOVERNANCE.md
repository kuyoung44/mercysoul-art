# MercySoul Advertising Governance

**Status:** Approved baseline  
**Principle:** NORMAL_AD_LOAD → NEVER_EXCEED → PROTECT_CORE_FUNCTIONS → USER_CONTROL

## Rules
1. Advertising must remain within a normal, basic-platform advertising experience.
2. Ad frequency, density, repetition, and interruption must not become excessive.
3. Advertising must never obstruct essential product functions, navigation, creation, messaging, accessibility, security, or core workflows.
4. Ads must remain clearly distinguishable from product content and system controls.
5. Avoid deceptive placements, forced ad interactions, abusive autoplay, or ad flooding.
6. User control and usability take priority over advertising intensity.
7. Any future advertising implementation or change must be checked against this principle before release.

This policy governs advertising behavior only; it does not authorize unrelated feature, security, infrastructure, or permission changes.
